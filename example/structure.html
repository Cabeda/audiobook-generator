The Lantern Makers

Lanterns were made by hand in the workshop at the end of the lane, and every part of the work had its own season.

Tools of the trade

The bench held a small set of tools, each one repaired many times over.

  • A pair of shears for cutting tin.
  • A wooden mallet for shaping the frame.
  • A tin of black paint and a narrow brush.

A week of work

The work ran to a rhythm that everyone in the shop knew by heart.

  1. Monday: cut and fold the frames.
  2. Wednesday: fit the glass panes.
  3. Friday: paint and hang the lanterns.

The best lantern is the one nobody notices is lit.

The Last Order

The last order of the winter came from the harbour, and it asked for more lights than the workshop had tin for.

The makers counted what they had and sent back a smaller number.
